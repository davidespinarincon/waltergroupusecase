Slow email quotes cost us half of all price requests

Problem
~50%

of price requests are lost because the answer arrives too late

  • 280 price requests a day by email
  • 45% arrive incomplete (weight, date missing)
  • Median reply: 4 h, or 26 h if we must ask back
  • Conversion of requests to orders: 12%
Solution

An AI workflow for the pricing inbox

  • Reads every email and extracts the 4 required fields
  • Asks the customer when data is missing
  • Gets the price from the pricing API and replies
  • Hands exceptions to the team, with the reason

The LLM understands, rules decide.

Case 1: what I would clarify before building

Questions
  • How many emails a day, and how many are price requests?
  • How many arrive incomplete, and which field is missing?
  • How fast do we reply today, and how many orders do we lose by replying late?
  • What is a “standard” request: only full truck loads? Without dangerous or temperature-controlled goods?
  • May a quote go out without a person approving it? Who is accountable if it is wrong?
  • Can we look up a customer’s earlier orders?
  • What does validUntil mean? In the example it ends on 10.10., before the 14.10. pick-up.
Data
  • 3 months of the pricing inbox
  • 200 emails labelled by hand: type, missing fields, outcome
  • Reply time and conversion per request, from the CRM
  • Pricing API logs: how often it is down
Goal: a measured baseline and agreed targets before writing any code.

A person takes over when
  • Not a price request (e.g. invoices)
  • Extraction confidence below 0.8
  • A reference cannot be resolved
  • Over 24 t, past date or quote above €5,000
  • Pricing API down for 30 min or rejects the request

24 t, €5,000 and 30 min are my assumptions, to confirm with the pricing desk.
